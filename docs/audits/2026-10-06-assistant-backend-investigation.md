## 1. Assessment

The backend has several independent failures. A model replacement alone cannot fill the corpus or repair periods. A search integration alone cannot repair ranking, duplication, interpretation validation or deployment recovery. Persistent intelligence is useful, but persisting an unchecked interpretation currently makes an error reusable.

The recommended direction is a **bounded hybrid**, extending the existing database, ingestion stages and research queue: scheduled authoritative/source-specific ingestion; optional search for identified gaps; explicit historical coverage recovery; reusable, versioned company intelligence; and a smaller question-specific chat projection. This is a recommendation to validate, not a claim that the present digest implementation achieves it.

### Data failures — confirmed

- Public observed corpus: **6,244 announcements, 1,391 reports, 184 news documents**. Only **16 reports have indexed chunks**: 8/507 annual and 8/882 quarterly; neither interim report is indexed. Retained reports cannot contribute narrative context until indexed. Existing numeric extraction and narrative indexing are separate operations.
- News history is discontinuous: one dated document each in July 2024, February 2025, January–March 2026; 15 in May, 20 June, 16 July, 51 August, 15 September and 42 October 2026. **20 are undated**. Counts describe stored documents, not total real-world news or completeness.
- No `Document.symbol`-labelled news for LUCK or FFC. They have reports and announcements. This does not establish zero relevant articles: tags/text/indirect context can link differently. It does establish that directly labelled company-news coverage is absent in this sample.
- **129/184 news documents have no evidence tags**. All retained reports and announcements are untagged in this database. Company retrieval can still use symbols, but sector/topic lanes require tags and therefore miss much of the older corpus. Code that tags newly indexed documents does not retroactively fix these rows.
- The ingestion indexer uses `announcement` for PSX and `news` for every other evidence source. The observed news store consequently includes PBS, OFAC, Ministry of Finance, SECP and other official material. Independent of that storage classification, the search tool omits `document_type` and defaults `evidence_kind` to `reporting`; annual reports and transmission notices become entries in snapshot `news`. These are two separate classification failures. [Durable index stage](../../apps/api/app/services/evidence_operations.py#L594), [search envelope](../../apps/api/app/tools/research_tools.py#L79).
- Selected financial rows can retain suspect reporting periods/bases. LUCK's standardized rows include annual labels at December 31 with unknown period starts and accounting bases, alongside source-grounded June-year-end facts. The same net income value `33092162000.00000000` appears in a sourced standalone June 2025 annual and an unbased standardized December 2025 annual. This is a reconciliation flag, not proof that either source should be silently overwritten. FFC also contains source conflicts. `quality_status='observed'` is not semantic certification. [Selection](../../apps/api/app/services/company_snapshot.py#L42).

### Retrieval failures — confirmed mechanisms and observed examples

1. **Generic intent replaces relevant context.** Snapshot query classification returns only `earnings`. The LUCK chat question returns `investment`. In [the broader-lane rule](../../apps/api/app/services/news_retrieval.py#L75), either prevents the fallback `rates/inflation/fx/geopolitics` topics from being used. “Sector macro risks” in the snapshot query does not explicitly request those topics. The snapshot selects two ArcBest US trucking passages; chat selects a Chinese fertilizer expansion story. Neither has a supplied LUCK transmission mechanism.
2. **Keyword admission is too permissive for company relevance.** [RAG ranking](../../apps/api/app/services/rag_service.py#L687) raises lexical score to the admission threshold for any requested topic-term match. Tagged semantic candidates can also pass without ordinary lexical overlap. Company/sector/topic membership plus a generic word is weaker than question relevance, geography or an evidenced business exposure. Interleaving then spends scarce slots on admitted results rather than comparing their marginal usefulness.
3. **Diversity is coarse.** Six snapshot passages interleave three lanes. There is no required coverage of earnings drivers, expansion, dividends, company developments, macro and contrary evidence, nor a mechanism for identifying a missing topic before filling its slot. Per-document limits allow two weak passages from the same story. Useful geopolitical documents are already stored and tagged, including Iraq/Hormuz and energy/shipping developments, but none appears in the inspected six-passage LUCK snapshot. Their company impact remains an interpretation requiring support.
4. **Deduplication has mismatched identities.** Search deduplicates chunk IDs and caps each document at two passages. It does not collapse overlapping paragraphs or syndicated stories at selection time. Ingestion has URL/body hashes and bounded SimHash checks, so it is incorrect to say there is no deduplication anywhere. Those protections do not establish unique model-facing evidence. [Ingestion duplicate checks](../../apps/api/app/services/evidence_pipeline.py#L363), [selection](../../apps/api/app/services/news_retrieval.py#L89).
5. **No default temporal separation.** Snapshot/news calls have no date window. Historical reports, transmission notices and current articles compete without explicit “current developments” versus “historical context” roles. One FFC selection is a notice explicitly titled `REVOKED`; semantic source status is not used to gate that selection.

### Payload failures — confirmed

- `research.company_digest` supplies **the underlying snapshot plus the brief**, not just a reusable concise assessment. A short output does not imply short input.
- [Brief projection](../../apps/api/app/services/research_generation_service.py#L308) interns source documents and preserves compact source locations. [Chat digest assembly](../../apps/api/app/tools/research_tools.py#L315) removes that source table and attaches full saved source dictionaries. [Packet fusion](../../apps/api/app/ai/company_packet.py#L109) carries those dictionaries forward; it expands data to merge it and later recompacts it. Expansion alone is not the root cause: additional provenance, content and differing source identities are.
- Full source identity is compared as serialized dictionaries. Different IDs, quotes or metadata for the same document/page produce separate E references. This is sometimes necessary to preserve distinct evidence spans; repeated document IDs/titles/URLs are not necessary on each span.
- Search text deduplication tracks search-delivered chunk IDs, not snapshot-delivered passages. The same asset-finance chunk is sent in the digest and again in search under E28/E56. The same document/page has E28/E55/E56. The saved snapshot and live `market.latest` also overlap, although the fresh quote remains necessary if the snapshot quote is old.
- All **25 tool definitions** are provided for this ordinary question. Portfolio and IPS reads are justified by the selected-portfolio investment request, but full provenance and unrelated calculation details need not be transmitted to explain the mandate.

### Model, interpretation and citation failures — confirmed

The LUCK saved brief calls falling PKR-per-USD “depreciation,” converts an absolute FX difference into a percentage, calls operating-profit growth margin growth, and attributes revenue changes to volume/pricing without supplied supporting statements. It also treats dated inflation context as a favorable current environment. The chat checkpoint contains the incorrect saved FX claim beside the correct numerical evidence; the earlier live audit records the repeated error. These are distinct from retrieval irrelevance.

The company-brief validator checks schema, allowed reference IDs and word count. It does **not** check numerical claims, direction, period, attribution, or entailment. The older event-digest validator has additional numerical checks, but those do not apply to `company_brief`. `kind='reported'` is a model declaration, not a verified classification. [Validator](../../apps/api/app/services/research_generation_service.py#L124).

Citation identity validation cannot certify the claim. Moreover, some macro snapshot sources retain only series name/release time, losing publisher/URL/artifact linkage in the snapshot's source-cleaning step. LUCK S41 has no URL. The resolver recognizes individual `[[E1]]` markers; grouped `[[E25], [E47], [E50]]` syntax is not resolved. Inline `[S41]` survives in saved brief text even when structured refs are remapped. [Resolver](../../apps/api/app/ai/tool_loop.py#L746), [macro evidence](../../apps/api/app/services/context_builder.py#L988).

The evidence does not show that one provider fixes everything. Prior company-packet trials found better citation/basis handling with Anthropic and failures with GLM, but questions, outputs and payloads differ. Current stored Anthropic brief attempts include failed/truncated and uncertain outcomes. No new model evaluation was authorized, so neither latency nor quality superiority is established for a repaired design.

Bare system text is **737** local tokens; bare user content is **23,779**, predominantly the packet. The complete request also includes **25 tools** and their provider-template formatting. Removing tools in a local template ablation reduces the safety-margined count by **4,867**; removing the packet source registry reduces it by **6,960**. These are diagnostic upper bounds, **not** recommendations to remove evidence or tools wholesale. Removing one component can alter neighboring tokenization; these deltas should not be added into a promised savings figure.

Source-field samples contain 1,040 tokens of document IDs, 949 of URLs, 720 of IDs and 960 of price provenance, before surrounding structure. Multiple refs point at identical document/pages: five refs at annual page 292 and six at interim page 7. Keep source-row/spatial distinctions but intern the document once. Share financial period/basis/unit headers without shortening decimals or losing reporting definitions. Token efficiency should come from reducing redundant representation and selecting useful content, not rounding exact financial values or cutting qualifications.

Latency is not purely a token issue. In the inspected chat, individual backend reads measured **4.217 s IPS/compliance**, **2.529 s search**, **1.179 s digest**, **0.244 s portfolio summary** and **0.079 s latest quote**. Reads can run concurrently, so these must not be summed as elapsed time. Provider latency was **40.089 s**. Prior audits measured roughly 16 seconds for cold embedding initialization versus subsecond warmed examples; startup preload already addresses that cold path. Smaller provider inputs and faster backend projections should be evaluated separately, with matched answers and provider settings. External live search adds another latency stage rather than automatically shortening this path.

## 4. Viable approaches, including Tavily

| Need / approach | Advantages | Costs, limits and suitability |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Scheduled publisher feeds + official adapters | Predictable recurring coverage, original artifacts, source-specific dates and reproducible ingestion; existing stages already support it | RSS is shallow history; adapter maintenance and title-filter false negatives; coverage needs monitoring. Best baseline for verified sources. |
| Scheduled search discovery, including Tavily | Can discover companies/topics missing from the registered publisher set | Recurring paid queries, search-index bias and bounded ranked results; not a complete archive. Worth testing as targeted gap filling rather than automatically sweeping every company. |
| Live search on every chat | Freshness on demand and little precomputed corpus requirement | Adds external latency/cost, repeats work across users, weakens reproducibility and can return unsupported snippets. Does not satisfy opening-company reuse alone. |
| Conditional live search + stored baseline | Fast reuse with freshness/gap fallback; captures discovered material for later use | Needs explicit coverage/age triggers, timeout behavior and ingestion validation; can still miss unknown gaps. Recommended candidate. |
| Publisher archives/sitemaps/date-page recovery | Explicit window/page accounting; reproducible partial/completed coverage | Parsing, pagination, paywalls and unavailable pages; slower than top-result search. Best route where archive contracts work. |
| Historical search/GDELT/Tavily | Helpful discovery when archives are missing or broken | Search top results cannot certify completeness; current GDELT is unavailable. Record “searched” separately from “archive exhausted.” |
| Licensed news/archive provider | Potentially stronger entity/date coverage and support | License/retention cost, PSX company recall unknown; requires a vendor trial. Viable if measured gaps persist. |
| Rebuild company context on every question | Avoids treating a stored interpretation as authority; flexible evidence | Repeated latency/tokens and unstable selections; useful baseline for deep questions, poor default reuse. |
| Monolithic saved AI brief | Simple UI and amortized generation | Error propagation, coarse invalidation and inadequate support for new questions; insufficient by itself. |
| Versioned evidence-backed sections/claims + compact brief | Reusable intelligence with dates, dependencies, fact IDs, contrary evidence and auditability; structured facts remain independent | More dependency/quality engineering and fragment reconciliation; useful target without requiring a knowledge graph. |
| Full knowledge graph / agentic research at ingestion | May help complex cross-company relationships | Significant extraction, maintenance and model cost; no evidence here justifies making it a prerequisite. Evaluate only after simpler relevance/claim models fail. |

Tavily is **not integrated in this backend**. Its documented Search offers bounded results, domain/date filters and selectable depth; higher depth trades latency for relevance. Date estimates may reflect updates, and undated results survive date filtering unless explicitly excluded. None of this establishes Pakistani company recall or historical completeness. Use it as candidate discovery, verify publisher/date/body, and preserve the original source. Avoid ingesting generated answers as facts. [Search contract](https://docs.tavily.com/documentation/api-reference/endpoint/search).

For known selected URLs, Extract is a distinct option; inspect per-URL failures even with HTTP 200. Whether it improves extraction over current adapters needs a corpus trial. [Extract contract](https://docs.tavily.com/documentation/api-reference/endpoint/extract). Basic Search costs one credit, advanced two; Extract and Crawl add their own usage. Budget per useful unique accepted article, not per nominal search hit. [Credits](https://docs.tavily.com/documentation/api-credits).

A future trial should compare current discovery, Tavily basic/fast/advanced and archive discovery on the same labelled company/sector/geopolitical date windows. Test known-event recall, unique useful sources, exact dates, source body access, latency and cost. Keep original source types; do not turn every result into `news`. A snippet-only result should carry an explicit evidence-depth gap. No Tavily coverage or latency estimate was measured in this investigation.

## 5. Recommended scope, trade-offs and validation

**First increment: observability and evidence correctness.** Retain a bounded encrypted diagnostic sample of the exact provider boundary for designated audit cases, with artifact/version IDs and section counts; prevent sampled successes from disappearing before investigation. Reconcile suspicious financial periods/bases and numeric source attribution for the pilot set; distinguish report, announcement, policy, article and commentary; restore source links. Treat existing saved interpretations as unverified, and quarantine confirmed incorrect claims. Trade-off: some answers become more explicit about insufficiency before coverage improves.

**Second increment: retrieval and projection.** Pilot LUCK, FFC and contrasting sectors on a labelled question set. Retrieve explicit company developments plus supported sector/business exposures and independently relevant macro/geopolitical context. Separate current and historical lanes; allow empty slots. Select unique stories/passages by usefulness, preserve counterevidence, and support deeper retrieval automatically. Assemble a compact question-specific view of dated intelligence, necessary exact facts, fresh developments and selected-portfolio mandate/calculations. Intern citation documents and source spans across digest/search/market/portfolio results; keep detail tools available. Trade-off: a smaller first pass occasionally needs a second read; evaluate total turn cost and latency, not only initial JSON size.

**Third increment: reliable recurring ingestion and historical recovery.** Design a persistent desired-service state and explicit deployment pause/drain/recreate/resume gate with singleton producer ownership, source-lag alerts and durable candidate ledgers. Resume only after a separate authorized operational change; nothing was restarted here. Use verified live lanes without waiting for complete historical recovery. Recover retained report text incrementally and publisher windows with recorded omissions; replace blocked GDELT dependence where an alternative is verified. Preserve live capacity. Trade-off: bounded backfill is slower but does not overwhelm live indexing or pretend capped work is exhaustive.

**Fourth increment: reusable company intelligence and targeted search.** Save company-only sections for financial performance, earnings drivers, expansion, dividends, material developments, sector/macro influences, risks and unresolved questions. Every material statement needs source/period/attribution, fact versus interpretation, dependencies and validation state. Update affected sections when evidence changes; time-sensitive freshness is evaluated separately from unchanged-source reuse. Keep quote/risk/macro overlays on their own cadence, and keep portfolio/IPS private and execution-specific. Add a bounded Tavily discovery/extraction pilot only if it improves coverage or latency-adjusted relevance over the baseline. Trade-off: more precise dependency tracking; repeated paid synthesis is avoided, but authoritative claims require stricter validation than schema acceptance.

Proposed acceptance criteria — targets to agree and measure, not achieved results:

| Dimension | Gate |
| --- | --- |
| Exactness | Zero altered values, currencies, units, periods/bases or silently mixed definitions in selected fact → snapshot → wire fixtures. Conflicts and unknown bases remain explicit. Zero source-unsupported numeric/directional claims in the adjudicated pilot. |
| Attribution/citations | Every material factual claim resolves to the original source/span/page/date; 100% citation resolution in pilot follow-ups; grouped markers either resolve or fail visibly. Management claims and interpretations are not certified as reported facts. |
| Relevance/coverage | Label company, sector and geopolitical questions independently. Initial target: ≥80% useful passages among the first five and ≥90% recall of the labelled material-event set where sources are accessible. Report unavailable and unindexed evidence separately. These are sampled metrics, never market-wide completeness claims. |
| Tokens | On the same ordinary LUCK task, target ≤10K provider input for the first call and ≥50% lower cumulative input across standard/follow-up tasks, while passing exactness/relevance gates. Aim for 3–5K company evidence on ordinary questions; deeper justified requests may exceed it explicitly. |
| Latency | Warm stored retrieval p95 under 2 seconds and comfortably within existing deadlines; do not regress time to first answer at matched quality/provider. Measure cold startup, ingestion queue delay and optional search separately. Live fallback must have a finite latency/cost budget and a source-gap response on failure. |
| Freshness/recovery | Verified sources polled within their configured SLA; accepted articles searchable within an initial 5-minute p95 target. Deployment restores intended services exactly once. Simulated interruption resumes without lost/duplicated work. Coverage ledger distinguishes fetched/indexed/exhausted/partial/unavailable. |
| Reuse | Unchanged inputs cause zero additional synthesis calls. Relevant correction invalidates the affected claim/section. Unrelated sector changes do not regenerate every company; source age can make an overlay stale without inventing a new observation. |
| Portfolio behavior | Ordinary investment/alternative questions automatically receive the selected owned portfolio, confirmed IPS, goals, required-return evidence and required deterministic calculation results. Allocation proposals remain subject to verification; no trade execution is introduced. |

Remaining uncertainties: PSX company-news recall and article access through Tavily; licensing costs; how much retained-report text requires OCR; actual provider quality on the repaired payload; false negatives from material-news filters; adequacy of dependency-driven section refresh; source reliability for suspect financial rows; and achievable end-to-end latency under concurrent load.
